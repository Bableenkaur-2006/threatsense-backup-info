Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

ThreatSense Backup Uploader is a personal backup utility operated by Bableen Kaur. It uses rclone on the owner's computer to transfer encrypted ThreatSense recovery archives to the owner's Google Drive.

Google data and permissions

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses file contents and metadata, such as names, sizes, identifiers and checksums, to upload, list and verify its backup files. If a retention policy is configured, it may also remove its own older backups. This permission limits access to files created or opened with this application.

Storage and sharing

Backup archives are encrypted before upload. Google stores the uploaded files in the owner's Drive. OAuth tokens used by rclone are stored in its local configuration; they are not published on this website. Access is used only for the personal backup workflow. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, shared with other users, or used to train AI models.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and can delete uploaded files in Google Drive. Access can be revoked through the Google Account third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously uploaded backups or local files.

This information website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, login forms or backup-upload forms. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process visitor information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Contact the operator through the developer's GitHub profile. Never post passwords, OAuth tokens or private backup files in a public issue.
